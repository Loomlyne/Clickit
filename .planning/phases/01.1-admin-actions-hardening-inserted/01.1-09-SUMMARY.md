# Phase 01.1 Plan 09: Show schedule core Summary

Bulk scheduling moved into a tested core: whole-or-nothing conditional updates with compensation, a slot-level audit record (previous and new status and publish_at for every episode), and an unschedule that reverts only slots the scheduler wrote and nobody changed since, refusing when its record is unreadable. Wiring is plan 11.
