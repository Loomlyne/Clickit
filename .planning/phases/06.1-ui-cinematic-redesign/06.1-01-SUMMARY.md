## Decisions Made

- ShowArt, SectionHeader, GlowBadge are server components — dynamic colors handled via inline styles, no hooks needed
- CinematicBackdrop and ScrollReveal require "use client" for animation (motion.div) and style computation
- Hex alpha suffix pattern (e.g. `${color}40`) for opacity variants rather than `rgba()` for concise readable inline styles
- glow-pulse keyframe lives in globals.css alongside the .animate-glow-pulse utility (co-located with usage pattern)
