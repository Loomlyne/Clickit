Cached public pages: what visitors see change

To fit Workers Free (10 ms CPU per request), every public page is built once and served from cache, the same for every visitor. Anything personal (who is signed in, My List, likes, progress, Continue watching) is filled in by the browser right after the page appears, usually within 0.1–0.3 s. Signed-out visitors see no change at all. Three choices below. In each, option A is the recommended one. Colours, sizes and copy come from the product (globals.css, en.json, ar.json); the logo and photos are stand-ins.

Phone / desktop split

Each page is kept twice, once for phones and once for desktops, because the landing hero picks one video by device. The detection is the same one the page uses today, so visitors see no difference. It only doubles the stored copies.

Admin edits visible

A change saved in Admin (text, ticker, shows, coming-soon switch) reaches signed-out visitors of these pages within 120 s instead of at once. The plan replaces this with "within about 10 s" by reading the last-save time that OpenNext already keeps in D1.

Also changing (no picture needed)

  • Episode resume: the player waits ~0.1–0.3 s for the saved position before it starts, so it still resumes at the right second.
  • Arabic pages: footer, FAQ and legal text follow the URL (/ar) instead of a cookie. This fixes a case where a first-time Arabic visitor could get English footer or FAQ text.
  • Admin edits (content, ticker, coming-soon switch) reach visitors within about 10 s instead of at once.
  • Hero video picks the phone or desktop file in the browser. A phone may show the poster image for a moment before the video starts.
